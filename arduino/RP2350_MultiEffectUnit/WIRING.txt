Power Supply:
=============
- Use USB power (5V) for RP2350
- RP2350 provides 3.3V for all peripherals
- Ensure adequate current capacity (500mA recommended)

Audio Connections:
==================
INPUT:
  Guitar/Instrument → 1/4" Jack → Audio Input Circuit → PCM1808

OUTPUT:
  PCM5102 → Audio Output Circuit → 1/4" Jack → Amplifier

Notes:
======
1. PCM1808 and PCM5102 require proper analog input/output circuitry
   (AC coupling capacitors, input/output impedance matching)

2. Add 0.1µF decoupling capacitors near VCC pins of all ICs

3. Keep I2S signal traces short and away from analog audio

4. Use proper ground plane for audio quality

5. Consider adding ferrite beads on power lines for noise reduction

6. PCM5102 SCK pin to GND enables auto clock mode
   PCM5102 FMT pin to 3.3V selects I2S format

Signal Levels:
==============
- Digital I/O: 3.3V logic
- Audio ADC: 2Vrms max input (configure with input attenuator)
- Audio DAC: 2Vrms output (configure with output buffer)

Pin Summary:
============
GPIO 4:  I2C SDA (OLED)
GPIO 5:  I2C SCL (OLED)
GPIO 6:  Encoder CLK
GPIO 7:  Encoder DT
GPIO 8:  Encoder SW
GPIO 20: I2S BCK (Bit Clock)
GPIO 21: I2S LRCK (Left/Right Clock)
GPIO 22: I2S TX (to DAC)
GPIO 26: I2S RX (from ADC)
